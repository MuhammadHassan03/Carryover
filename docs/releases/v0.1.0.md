First public preview of Carryover for Windows 10 and 11. Free, no account.

**What it does today (read-only preview)**

- Scans this PC into a PC Recipe: hardware, disks and BitLocker state, apps (with winget IDs), drivers including the storage driver Windows Setup needs, and your files (names and sizes only).
- Two modes: *Set up a new device* and *Refresh this PC* (fresh Windows without a USB stick).
- Builds a plan fitted to the target machine and checks it against 13 safety rules. Try "Try a risky AI plan" to watch the checker block one.
- Optional *Organize my files*: tidy loose Desktop and Downloads files into folders. Never deletes or overwrites.
- Save your recipe as a file.

Nothing on your PC is changed yet: the Start button stays disabled until the step runner ships.

**Downloads**

- `Carryover_0.1.0_x64-setup.exe`: installer.
- `Carryover-0.1.0-portable.exe`: single file, no install. Needs WebView2, which Windows 11 already has.
- `SHA256SUMS.txt`: checksums.

The files aren't code-signed yet (signing through SignPath Foundation is on the way), so Windows SmartScreen may say "Windows protected your PC". Choose *More info*, then *Run anyway*. You can compare the checksum first.

For TPM state in the scan, click *Restart as administrator* in the app when it offers.
